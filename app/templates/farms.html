1. Moisture Deficit Ratio (Rdeficit):

Deficit ratio is calculated relative to root-zone Field Capacity (FC) and Wilting Point (WP): R = (Target% - Current%) / (Target% - WiltingPoint%). If current moisture ≥ target, deficit is 0.

2. Required Irrigation Water (Liters):

User inputs the baseline gross seasonal water need (in liters). The system adjusts for current soil moisture: Remaining Demand = Required Liters × Rdeficit.

3. Hydraulic Constraints:

All plot deliveries respect plot soil infiltration rate (mm/hr × area) to avoid surface runoff, ponding, or waterlogging.
