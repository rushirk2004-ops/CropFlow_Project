Algorithm Suite At-a-Glance
Greedy Allocation O(N log N)

Prioritizes critical flowering crops with highest urgency ratios until water runs out.

Dynamic Programming O(N × M × K)

Discretizes water volume and determines global maximum net yield on concave FAO-33 curves.

Hybrid Approach Proposed Standard

Guarantees vital survival baseline for sensitive plots, then optimizes residual water via DP.

Mathematical Problem Formulation

CropFlow addresses the bounded water deficit allocation problem with non-linear yield responses and hydraulic pump limits:

Objective Function:
Maximize Z = ∑i=1..N [ Ypres(wi) × Valuei ] - ∑i=1..N [ Costpumping(wi) + Costlabor(Ti) ]
Constraints:
1. ∑i=1..N wi ≤ Wavailable   (Water Budget Constraint)
2. 0 ≤ wi ≤ Demandi   (No Waterlogging / Waste)
3. Qi ≤ Infiltrationi × Areai   (Runoff Prevention Constraint)
4. FAO-33 Concave Yield: Y/Ymax = 1 - Ky × (1 - wi/Demandi)
Why the Hybrid Strategy Wins

Under severe drought (e.g. 50% water curtailment), Greedy allocates 100% to the top 2 plots and leaves remaining plots with 0%, causing complete crop death in those fields.

Hybrid guarantees a 25-30% baseline to prevent irreversible wilting across all sensitive crops, and uses Dynamic Programming to find the optimal diminishing-return balance for the remaining water.
