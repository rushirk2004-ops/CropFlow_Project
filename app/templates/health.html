1. Dynamic Programming \(O(N \cdot M \cdot K)\)

• \(N\) = Number of agricultural plots.
• \(M\) = Discretized water capacity units: \(M = \lfloor W_{\text{available}} / \Delta w \rfloor\).
• \(K\) = Feasible discrete allocation levels: \(K = \lceil D_{\max} / \Delta w \rceil\).

Guarantees Bellman optimality across the discrete resource lattice with concave FAO-33 yield response curves.

2. Hybrid Strategy \(O(N \log N + N \cdot M' \cdot K)\)

• \(N \log N\) = Greedy priority score sorting.
• \(M'\) = Residual water capacity after reserving vital survival anchors (\(M' \ll M\)).
• \(N \cdot M' \cdot K\) = DP optimization over reduced residual space.

Combines zero-starvation safety anchors for vulnerable flowering crops with exact DP yield maximization.

• \(N \log N\) = Priority queue triage sorting.
